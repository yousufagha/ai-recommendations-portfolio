What is in the Dubai data

Machine-readable list of every file: data-catalog.json.

Each record has its value in dirhams, area, project, building, property type, rooms, size, price per square metre, and nearest metro, mall and landmark. Rent contracts add annual amount, dates, size and usage, and a price index covers homes, flats and villas. Sales run mainly from 2008 and rents from 2014, both into 2026. Files are split by year, and manifests record row counts and checksums. Read the datasheet for fields and tips.

Dubai Golden Visa analysis

How someone with about AED 2 million can qualify for a UAE Golden Visa through property while making the most of their money.

First finding: demand gathers at the AED 2 million line, and no clear extra cost is proven. From Q2 2022, residential sales just above AED 2 million outnumber those just below it by 1.56 to 1, up from 1.10 to 1 in 2015 to 2021. A first reading of about AED 39,000 appears when similar-sized homes in the same building are compared, but prices with no visa line show swings of a similar size, so it is not claimed as a real cost.

So a buyer can choose on value and returns, and compare price per square metre within the building as a precaution. The visa link is inferred, because the records do not show who received one. Rankings of AED 2 million options are next. Read the story so far.
