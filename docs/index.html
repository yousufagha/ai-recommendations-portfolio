Dubai's market in numbers

Sales have reached record levels, and rents have recovered strongly since 2021. Together they set the income side of any investment case.

What happens at the AED 2 million line

Property worth AED 2 million or more can support a UAE Golden Visa application. So we asked: do buyers aim for that price, and does it cost them more?

What it means for a buyer with about AED 2 million

Reaching the line does not appear to carry a clear premium, so choose the home on value and returns. As a precaution, compare price per square metre with similar homes in the same building.

Would it have worked? The test on past years

Each November from 2022 to 2025, we picked five AED 2 million homes using only what was known that day, then followed them for the next year against the other ranked options.

What it means: use the ranking to choose for income

The ranking finds homes that earn more rent. It does not predict which homes will rise most in price: for total return, rent plus price change, the verdict is not shown. Picks were ahead in 3 of 4 rounds on both price measures, but the summed gap beat 95% of random picks on only one of them (16.9 points, against 3.8). Limited to well-measured options, the gaps nearly vanish after 2022.

Homes across all prices often earn a higher rental yield, and picks trailed all ready flats in 3 of 4 rounds, so the visa is part of what a threshold buyer gets for their money. Four rounds in one market cycle cannot prove much. This was not a blind test: a reviewer ran the original plan before the method was final, and the decisions log records what changed.
