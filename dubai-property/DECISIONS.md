# Decisions

- 2026-11-05: This project comes first, because the UAE is needed sooner.
- 2026-11-05: The headline is the AED 2 million Golden Visa question; the fair-price model is a measuring tool, not the deliverable.
- 2026-11-05: Objective: AI recommendations that show in dirhams who profits and by how much, then test whether they would have worked.
- 2026-11-05: "Us" is an advisory firm selling this analysis to investors and developers.
- 2026-11-05: Report in Tableau Public; only area-level summaries are uploaded, because viewers can download published data.
- 2026-11-05: Row-level access is dropped, because a public report cannot show it; Superset stays a possible add-on.
- 2026-11-05: Publish the paper-investment result even if the engine loses to the market or the simple rule.
- 2026-11-05: Official sources conflict on visa length and mortgages; the report shows the conflict rather than choosing.
- 2026-11-05: Claude memo text is generated once and stored, and fails if any number differs from the results file.
- 2026-11-05: Scope is the property decision only, with separate rankings for living in the home and renting it out.
- 2026-11-05: AI results are computed in advance, because Tableau Public cannot call AI live; viewers explore them with filters.
- 2026-11-05: Raw transactions are public in the repo, split by year and gzipped, because GitHub refuses files over 100 MB.
- 2026-11-05: data.dubai is the source for history, because the Land Department page only reaches back to January 2026.
- 2026-11-05: Test value at AED 2M against other round prices, nearby prices and earlier years, because prices cluster at round numbers anyway.
- 2026-11-05: Treat the value step at AED 2M as unproven, because prices with no visa line show steps of similar size. No saving is claimed from it.
- 2026-11-05: The landing page is built from a template and the results files by `pipeline/build_site.py`, and tests fail if a written claim stops holding, so charts cannot drift from the analysis.
- 2026-11-05: Dashboards are Tableau Public for Dubai and Looker Studio for Grid Pulse. Superset is dropped because a public link would need a paid server.
- 2026-11-05: Market rent uses new contracts only, because renewals lag: 2025 flats were AED 971 per sqm new against AED 693 renewed.
- 2026-11-05: Rent is estimated per sale from the same project, then same-price projects, then the area adjusted for price, because all-price area rents overstated AED 2M yields (cold review: Burj Khalifa 2-bed about 7.9% against about 6.0%).
- 2026-11-05: Rank only ready flats with at least half their sales on project evidence, in tiers set by the leave-one-project-out error. Off-plan and villas stay unranked.
- 2026-11-05: Call results "homes priced at the threshold". u.ae confirms AED 2M capital and a 5-year term for real estate but not off-plan, mortgage or joint rules, and secondary sources conflict, so recommendations must not state those as fact.
- 2026-11-05: The backtest is not blind. The cold reviewer ran the original plan to size problems and reported 2 wins of 5 ("not shown"). Every later change was made after seeing that. Our own replications give 4 of 5 and 3 of 5 depending on weights, so the original rule's result depends on an unstated detail. All are recorded in `validation/backtest.json` or here.
- 2026-11-05: After the preview, the test became picks against non-picks, realised rent, pooled price change, two price windows and a permutation test. 2021 is no test, because the picks were the whole set.
- 2026-11-05: After seeing results, added two checks: options with 100+ matched sales, because two thin options alone produced the +9.55 point gap in 2025; and a rent-only permutation test. The verdict rule stayed the same, and the verdict stayed "not shown".
- 2026-11-05: The product claim is limited to rent: picks earned 0.5 to 0.7 points more out of sample. No claim on total return or price prediction.
- 2026-11-05: Buy-and-hold uses a 4% fee from the [Dubai Land Department fee table](https://rdc.dubailand.gov.ae/Assets/img/Real_Estate_Legislation_en.pdf). AED 200 per sqm service charge is an unverified assumption.
- 2026-11-05: Tableau reads flat CSVs built from `validation/` by `pipeline/build_tableau.py`, with no new analysis, so the dashboard cannot disagree with tested results.
- 2026-11-06: Present the backtest rent first: "Rent edge: delivered in 4 of 4 rounds. Total-return edge: not shown, so we don't claim one." "Delivered", not "proven", because four rounds cannot prove much. The verdict label, every number and "trailed all ready flats in 3 of 4" stay as they are.
- 2026-11-08: The findings section leads with the tested rent result as one chart (paired bars, gap in points) plus three ledger rows. Reviewed against the unslop checklist (heydamir/unslop, CC BY 4.0). The same-price rent claim is a range across AED 2M to 2.2M homes, not a like-for-like gap.
- 2026-11-08: Page palette is Sand and ink (warm paper, ink hero, copper and teal series). A Theme button cycles Auto, Light and Dark and remembers the choice. The Tableau dashboard still uses blue and grey.
- 2026-11-09: Restored the original navy and blue palette on the page, so the page, the Tableau dashboard and the social graphics share one brand. The Theme button stays. The Sand and ink palette of 2026-11-08 is retired.
