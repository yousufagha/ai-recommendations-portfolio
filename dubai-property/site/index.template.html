What it means: use the ranking to choose for income

Rent edge: delivered in {{rounds_more_rent}} of {{rounds_tested}} rounds. Total-return edge: {{verdict}}, so we don't claim one. The ranking is built to find homes that earn more rent, and it is not used to predict which homes will rise most in price. Picks were ahead in {{total_rounds_ahead}} of {{rounds_tested}} rounds on both price measures, but the summed gap beat 95% of random picks on only one of them ({{sum_plan}} points, against {{sum_centred}}). Limited to well-measured options, the gaps nearly vanish after 2022.

Homes across all prices often earn a higher rental yield, and picks trailed all ready flats in {{wider_behind}} of {{rounds_tested}} rounds, so the visa is part of what a threshold buyer gets for their money. Four rounds in one market cycle cannot prove much. This was not a blind test: a reviewer ran the original plan before the method was final, and the decisions log records what changed.
